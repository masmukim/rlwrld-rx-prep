## 핵심 사실
- "The Company manufactures automotive wire harnesses." 주요 고객 Hyundai Motor, Kia.
- 주주(2025-12-31): Kyungshin Holdings 50.0%, Sumitomo Electric Industries 30.0%, Sumitomo Wiring Systems 20.0%.
- 제품: Wiring Harness, FPC Module, Junction Block, Wire to Wire Connector 등 (하네스 외 제품군 있음).
- 연혁: 1984-10 Gyeongju Plant에서 승용차용 와이어 하네스 생산 시작. 2000-03 Hwaseong 신공장. 2003-08 Gunsan 신공장. 2005-05 본사·연구소 인천 이전. 2010 커넥터 사업 진출.
- 1997-07 ISO9001 취득 문구에 "All wire harness plants"가 쓰임.

## 원문 발췌
> Oct. 1984 The Company began production of wire harnesses for passenger cars at Gyeongju Plant.
> Mar. 2000 The Company set up a new plant in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do.
> Aug. 2003 The Company set up a new plant in Gunsan, Jeollabuk-do.
> Sumitomo Electric Industries, Ltd. 30.0 / Sumitomo Wiring Systems Ltd. 20.0
