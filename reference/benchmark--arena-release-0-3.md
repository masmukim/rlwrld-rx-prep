## 핵심 사실
- Arena 0.3 기능: 조합형 환경, 환경 변이(변이 스윕), GPU 병렬 평가, 다중 노드 평가, 정책 클라이언트-서버(GR00T, π0.5 등), Subtask Predicates(단계별 마일스톤 추적), 민감도 분석.
- 생태계: "RLWRLD DexBench"를 포함해 벤치마크 저자들과 Arena에서 벤치마크를 구축·실행·오픈소스화하는 작업을 하고 있다고 서술.
- 다음 계획: Newton 기반 평가, 접촉 위주 삽입·조립, 소프트바디 pick-and-place, 케이블 라우팅 참조 평가.

## 원문 발췌
> We're working with benchmark authors and model developers to build, run, and open-source benchmarks on Arena, including Lightwheel RoboFinals, RLWRLD DexBench, UC Berkeley, RoboTwin, RoboDojo, NVIDIA GEAR's G1 Factory benchmark, X Square, Sharpa, and more. (raw: reference/raw/benchmark--arena-release-0-3.txt)

> Newton: Newton-based evaluation as a first-class workflow, with framework support and reference evaluations for contact-rich insertion and assembly, soft-body pick-and-place, and cable routing. (raw: reference/raw/benchmark--arena-release-0-3.txt)
