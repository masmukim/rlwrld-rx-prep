## 핵심 사실
- Physics Sim Lab, SpaceAI, NdotLight와 각각 MOU. DexBench(https://dexbench.org)를 고도화하기 위함.
- DexBench를 "NVIDIA와 협력해 open-source project로 개발 중이며 Isaac Lab-Arena 환경에 통합"되는 벤치마크로 서술. "18 Key Atomic Tasks and 80 representative use cases", 시뮬레이션과 실세계에서 정량 평가.
- 핵심 생태계 파트너: Lotte, SK Telecom, CJ Logistics, Hyosung, HL Mando, Fuji, ANA, Mitsui Chemicals.
- NdotLight: 시뮬레이션용 3D 자산 생성(TRINIX). Physics Sim Lab: 디지털 트윈 환경과 도메인 랜덤화, 합성 데이터로 RLDX-1 학습. SpaceAI: Deformable and Fragile Object Track(두부, 과일, 고무 부품), 4층 데이터(접촉력, 내부 응력, RGB, Depth).
- RLWRLD가 실세계 테스트베드로 시뮬레이션-실세계 충실도를 검증한다고 서술.

## 원문 발췌
> Built around 18 Key Atomic Tasks and 80 representative use cases, DexBench quantitatively evaluates whether robots can perform complex manipulation tasks with human-like dexterity across both simulation and real-world environments. (raw: reference/raw/benchmark--rlwrld-news-94.txt)

> DexBench is RLWRLD's benchmark framework for evaluating humanoid dexterity, being developed in collaboration with NVIDIA as an open-source project integrated into the NVIDIA Isaac Lab-Arena environment. (raw: reference/raw/benchmark--rlwrld-news-94.txt)
